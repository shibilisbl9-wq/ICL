Pill badges in tracked caps: the Season 2 mark, LIVE state, boundary/milestone callouts and qualification status.

- Consumer provides: a `<span>` with `icl-badge` and one modifier, 1–2 words of text.
- `icl-badge--season` repeats the logo's tagline: text "Season" with the numeral wrapped in `<b>` (drawn in `boundary-gold`).
- `icl-badge--live` always says LIVE; the dot alone is not enough.
- `icl-badge--gold` is for 4, 6, 50, 100, 5-wicket and trophy moments only. Never more than one gold badge per card.
