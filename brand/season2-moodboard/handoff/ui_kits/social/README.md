# ICL Season 2 — Social poster kit

Seven 1080×1350 feed templates composed from the design-system components: season announcement, countdown, match day, player of the match, squad/walkout, fixtures, thank-you.

- `index.html` — poster studio: pick a template, edit its copy live.
- `posters.jsx` — the templates. Each is `<Template w={displayWidth} d={data}/>`.

Player cutouts render as dashed placeholders until a `cutout` src is supplied. Team names, dates and venue are placeholders.
