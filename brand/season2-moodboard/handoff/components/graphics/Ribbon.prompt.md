Motion ribbon that trails the athlete. Arcs for bat swings and bowling actions, streaks for running between wickets.

```jsx
<Ribbon variant="arc" size={1400} width={180} style={{position:"absolute",left:-220,top:650}} />
<Ribbon variant="streak" size={600} width={120} colors={["var(--team-first-legends)","#fff","var(--icl-green)"]} />
```

- Colours: the player's team kit + exactly one brand stripe (green or orchid).
