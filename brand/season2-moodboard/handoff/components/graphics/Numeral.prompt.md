Giant display numeral — the single number that carries a poster's story (days to go, jersey, season 02). Put it behind a PlayerCutout.

```jsx
<Numeral size={860}>02</Numeral>
<Numeral size={1050} color="var(--icl-green)">3</Numeral>
```

- `size` in px on the 1080×1350 canvas. Keep ≈65% of poster height.
- One numeral per poster. Green on light and dark grounds; ink only on quiet posters.
