Rules of play

Check every post

Run each poster against these before it goes out. If a rule and a reference disagree, the rule wins.

Do

  • One number, one player, one motion device. That is the whole hierarchy.
  • Pick one ground per post: Crease, Red or Blue. Navy is for night and signal posts.
  • Break a frame on every post. A window, a tile, a numeral or the headline.
  • Set every fact in Mono, caps, with "+" or "·" between items.
  • Write scores the cricket way: 104* (58), 3/18 (4), 18.4 ov.

Don't

  • Never set red text on blue or blue on red. At 1.7:1 it vibrates and can't be read. They meet as shapes only.
  • No orange, green or purple in season artwork. The palette is final.
  • No full-colour gradient logo on a poster. Single colour only.
  • No drop shadows on type or cards. Contact shadows under players only.
  • No emoji, hashtags or exclamation marks on the artwork. Scale does the shouting.

Open before production

Team kits

Every frame uses one stand-in kit: electric blue with red and white trim. Swap in the real Season 2 team colours once kits are confirmed; red and blue stay the league's colours.

Real players

All faces are AI stand-ins. Shoot real players full body on a plain light-grey wall with hard light, to match the cut-out brief.

Logo colour

The green and purple lockup sits outside the red and blue palette. Decide whether posters stay single colour, as this board assumes, or the logo gets a red and blue Season 2 variant.

Text inside frames

Several generated frames carry AI-set words and invented placeholder names (the Swiss team sheet, the squad line-up). Reset all copy in the real fonts before anything is posted.
