Rules of play

Check every poster against these

DO

  • One numeral, one script phrase per poster. That's the whole hierarchy.
  • Cutouts on contact shadows. Feet below the numeral baseline.
  • Mono for every fact. Dates, times, grounds, scores, all caps, "+" or "·" between.
  • Team colours in panels, swatches and ribbons, never in headlines.
  • Crosshair footer on everything, including stories.

DON'T

  • No stadium skies, lens flares or glowing frames. That was Season 1.
  • No drop shadows on type or cards. No second gradient.
  • No emoji, hashtags or exclamation marks on the artwork.
  • No script on its own. It always crosses caps.
  • Flare stays under 4%. Circled dates, the final, LIVE, nothing else.
